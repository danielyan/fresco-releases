Fresco 0.4.2

• Update reminders: when a new version is out, a dot appears on the menu-bar icon and "Update Available" at the top of the menu, with a notification if you allow them.
• Settings → General → Updates: check automatically, install automatically, and Check Now.
• New shortcuts: ⌃⌥⌘P puts the previous wallpaper back, ⌃⌥⌘S saves the photo on the display under the pointer. Each can be turned off in Settings → Shortcuts.
• About Photo now appears for every online source, with links to the photo's page, its original source, author and license where available.
