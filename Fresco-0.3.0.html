Fresco 0.3.0

Fresco 0.3.0 — a face of its own.

Fresco has a proper icon now: a fanned stack of photos, in Finder,
notifications and this update window, with a matching symbol in the
menu bar that follows light and dark mode.

- Previewing wallpapers: Back and Forward walk through the candidates
  you've looked at, and Back can return to the wallpaper already on
  your desktop
- The menu opens faster: the preview is a small cached copy rather
  than the full-size image, and History no longer rebuilds each time
- The version line in the menu stays short on development builds
