Fresco 0.4.4

• New Schedule tab in Settings: how often the wallpaper changes, and switching mixes by time of day, in one place.
• New About tab, opened from "About Fresco" in the menu. The version now lives there instead of in the menu.
• Settings opens at a size that fits, and no longer resizes between tabs.
• Local Folder says "Folder not found" when its folder was moved or deleted.
• Wikimedia Commons, DeviantArt and Flickr have been removed.
