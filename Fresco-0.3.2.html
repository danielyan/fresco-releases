Fresco 0.3.2

Fresco 0.3.2 — fewer clicks.

- Change wallpaper from anywhere with ⌃⌥⌘N, without opening the menu.
  The shortcut is shown on Change Now, and can be turned off in
  Settings → General → Keyboard
- Never see a photo again: the ✕ beside Set on a previewed wallpaper
  blocks it for good and loads another
- The menu now says where wallpapers come from, e.g. "Wallhaven — NSFW",
  just above About Photo and History
