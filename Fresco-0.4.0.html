Fresco 0.4.0

Fresco 0.4.0 — mixes, and wallpapers for every display.

- Draw from several sources and categories at once. Tick them in the
  new Settings → Mix tab, or straight from the Sources menu (hold ⌥ to
  pick just one). Fresco picks a source at random, then one of its
  categories
- Save a mix by name ("Calm", "Space") and switch to it from the
  Sources menu, on any display
- Each display now keeps its own settings: mix, Light/Dark matching,
  minimum resolution and more. "Copy to All Displays" shares them
- Each display keeps its own wallpaper history, too
- Ultrawide and other unusual screens get photos shaped like them,
  instead of ones cropped hard to fill. Turn it off, or show the whole
  photo with matching bars instead of cropping, in Settings → General
- Displays are recognised reliably across docking, sleep and restarts
- Unsplash photos are fetched 30 at a time, so you hit its rate limit
  far less often
