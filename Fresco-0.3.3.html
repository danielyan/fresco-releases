Fresco 0.3.3

Fresco 0.3.3 — sharper wallpapers.

- Fresco now skips images too small for your display, so wallpapers no
  longer come out soft or blurry. Choose how strict in Settings →
  General → Minimum resolution: Standard (the default), Sharp for full
  Retina detail, or Any size
- With more than one display, each is judged by its own size in
  per-display mode, otherwise by the largest
- Local folders and Bing are never filtered
