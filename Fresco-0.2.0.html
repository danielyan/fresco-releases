Fresco 0.2.0

Fresco 0.2.0 — settings you can keep with your dotfiles.

Fresco's settings now live in ~/.config/fresco/config.json, and your
API keys in ~/.config/fresco/credentials.json (readable only by you).
Keep them alongside your other configuration and a new Mac can start
out set up the way you like it.

- Your current settings and keys move over automatically the first
  time 0.2.0 starts; there is nothing to do
- Edits to the files are picked up while Fresco is running
- Sign-ins to DeviantArt and Flickr stay on each Mac, so a new Mac
  asks you to connect those once
