Fresco 0.4.1

- Settings is reorganised: General, Mix, Displays, Sources and a new
  Shortcuts tab, each a simple form. Rotation options are now in Settings
  as well as the menu.
- When every display shares a wallpaper, they now share one set of
  settings too.
- Switch saved mixes by time of day: set a timetable in the Mix tab, such
  as bright photos in the morning and space at night.
